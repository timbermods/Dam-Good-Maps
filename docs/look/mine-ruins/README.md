# Mine sites and ruins: before and after (D178)

Kyler's small Map look round: mine sites and ruins as models of our own. Each view shows `dev`
before the change (left or top) and the branch `look/mine-site` after it (right or bottom), from
the same camera. Its sheet shows the after view in greyscale, protanopia, deuteranopia and
tritanopia. Our own renders only: River Valley, seed 4242, 256², and two test grounds the renderer
draws. Made with `npx tsx tools/capture-objects.ts`; the cameras are in [poses.json](poses.json).

## Mine sites

- [mine-close.jpg](mine-close.jpg) ([sheet](mine-close-sheet.jpg)): the game's default angle. Look at the dark earthen pit, the dull rusty frame, and the corner scaffolding with pale crates and planks.
- [mine-low.jpg](mine-low.jpg) ([sheet](mine-low-sheet.jpg)): a low angle. Look at the pit's depth, its banded walls, the roots and the ladder.
- [mine-top.jpg](mine-top.jpg) ([sheet](mine-top-sheet.jpg)): from above. Look at the 5 × 5 footprint: the pit in the middle 3 × 3 tiles, towers in the corners, beams over the edge.
- [overview.jpg](overview.jpg) ([sheet](overview-sheet.jpg)): the whole 256² map, Markers off. The insets enlarge a mine site and a ruin field 3 times, pixels as drawn: does each still read?
- [overview-markers.jpg](overview-markers.jpg) ([sheet](overview-markers-sheet.jpg)): the same with Markers on. Look at the orange outline round each mine site.
- [lineup-far.jpg](lineup-far.jpg) ([sheet](lineup-far-sheet.jpg)): the start, a badwater source, a mine site, a geothermal field, ruins, a water source and a relic, as far away as a whole 256² map. Does the mine site stay apart?
