## Intention

- A waterfall plunges off a cliff into a large, roughly round lake (Kyler's own). **Emerged** (a lake of 371 tiles of open water (axes 0.58, fill 0.4) with a fall of 7.3 levels into it).
- A waterfall shields the start: its cliff stands between the start and the nearest threat. **Dropped** (0 falls within 20 tiles; no threat on the map).

## How it plays

- You start by a river, 9 tiles away on your level.
- In the first drought it is gone by day 1: store water before it.
- A 9-tile dam 18 tiles south-east holds a drought's water.
- 1658 logs of wood within 20 tiles' walk, mostly oak: plenty of wood, slow to regrow; plus about 310 logs growing.
- A waterfall plunges off a cliff into a big, round lake.
- Expand on every side, on some level land.
- Further out: mine sites, a geothermal field, relics, other lakes and rivers, waterfalls and most of the ruins.

## Terrain

- **Land:** plateau, trough, escarpment, basin over land with no one regional slope.
- **Relief:** 14 levels (p5–p95), 9 levels in use, highest 15; 32% cliff, 49% flat; tallest fall 7.26 levels.
- **Water:** 5 rivers (0 from the edge, 5 from springs), 1 lake, 4 falls; water covers 7% of the map.
- **Reach:** 42% of the dry land is reached on foot from the start; 58% needs stairs (8 natural ramps, 0 uplands left to stairs).
- **Badwater:** none.
