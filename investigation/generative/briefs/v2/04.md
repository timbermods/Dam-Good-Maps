## Intention

- A signature landmark stands out: a spire, a mesa, a peak or a tall waterfall. **Emerged** (22 standing forms of 300 tiles or fewer (the most prominent 8 levels over its ring), tallest fall 7.8).
- A lake high on the heights spills over a fall. **Emerged** (a 2125-tile lake 6 levels above the map's middle spills over a fall).

## How it plays

- You start by a lake, 5 tiles away on your level.
- In the first drought it is gone by day 1: store water before it.
- An 8-tile dam 29 tiles west holds a drought's water.
- A landmark stands out: you can see it from anywhere on the map.
- A lake high on the heights spills over a fall.
- Expand to the north, west and south, on some level land.
- Further out: mine sites, a geothermal field, relics, another lake or river, waterfalls and most of the ruins.

## Terrain

- **Land:** basin, escarpment, mesaField, caldera, mesa over land falling toward the north-west.
- **Relief:** 9 levels (p5–p95), 13 levels in use, highest 13; 7% cliff, 42% flat; tallest fall 7.77 levels.
- **Water:** 4 rivers (1 from the edge, 3 from springs), 3 lakes, 2 falls; water covers 15% of the map.
- **Reach:** 28% of the dry land is reached on foot from the start; 72% needs stairs (0 natural ramps, 1 upland left to stairs).
- **Badwater:** none.
