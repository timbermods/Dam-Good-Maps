## Intention

- A hidden valley up the cliffs, reached only by stairs, holds riches. **Emerged** (2 uplands cut off by cliffs, reached only by stairs, with riches (largest 889 tiles)).
- A lake high on the heights spills over a fall. **Dropped** (no lake on the heights with a fall).

## How it plays

- You start by a stream, 3 tiles away on your level.
- It lasts through the first drought; in a 26-day Hard drought it is gone by day 4.
- A 2-tile dam 17 tiles south holds a drought's water.
- Thorns bar the way east, 27 tiles out.
- 141 logs of wood within 20 tiles' walk, mostly oak: plenty of wood, slow to regrow; plus about 70 logs growing.
- A valley up the cliffs holds riches: build stairs to reach it.
- Expand to the west and south, on narrow ledges.
- Most of the high ground needs stairs.
- Further out: mine sites, a geothermal field, relics, other lakes and rivers, waterfalls and most of the ruins.

## Terrain

- **Land:** basin, ridge, plateau over a bowl draining toward the south-east.
- **Relief:** 13 levels (p5–p95), 16 levels in use, highest 16; 29% cliff, 28% flat; tallest fall 3.1 levels.
- **Water:** 8 rivers (2 from the edge, 6 from springs), 3 lakes, 4 falls; water covers 22% of the map.
- **Reach:** 1% of the dry land is reached on foot from the start; 99% needs stairs (0 natural ramps, 6 uplands left to stairs).
- **Badwater:** none.

## Name
