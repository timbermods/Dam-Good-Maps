## Intention

- The best farmland lies past the gorge. **Dropped** (no larger farmland past a gorge (404 by the start)).
- Two rivers meet by the start. **Emerged** (a confluence 6 tiles from the start).

## How it plays

- You start by a river, 2 tiles away on your level.
- In the first drought it is gone by day 1: store water before it.
- A 14-tile dam 9 tiles south-west holds a drought's water.
- Thorns bar the way south-east, 22 tiles out.
- Two rivers meet by the start.
- Expand to the north and south, on narrow ledges.
- Most of the high ground needs stairs.
- Further out: mine sites, a geothermal field, relics, other lakes and rivers, waterfalls and most of the ruins.

## Terrain

- **Land:** ridge, mesa, trough over land with no one regional slope.
- **Relief:** 14 levels (p5–p95), 16 levels in use, highest 16; 15% cliff, 58% flat; tallest fall 3.96 levels.
- **Water:** 4 rivers (0 from the edge, 4 from springs), 1 lake, 5 falls, a delta; water covers 8% of the map.
- **Reach:** 4% of the dry land is reached on foot from the start; 96% needs stairs (3 natural ramps, 5 uplands left to stairs).
- **Badwater:** none.
