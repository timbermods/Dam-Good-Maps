## Intention

- The start sits under a cliff, with water below (Kyler's own). **Emerged** (75 tiles 2+ levels above within 7, cliff yes, water 4 tiles' walk).

## How it plays

- You start by a lake, 4 tiles away on your level.
- It lasts through the first drought; in a 26-day Hard drought it is gone by day 11.
- A 7-tile dam 18 tiles south-east holds a drought's water.
- You start under a cliff, with your water below.
- Expand to the east and north, on narrow ledges.
- Most of the high ground needs stairs.
- Further out: mine sites, a geothermal field, relics, waterfalls and most of the ruins.

## Terrain

- **Land:** basin, mesa, cone, escarpment over a bowl draining toward the south.
- **Relief:** 12 levels (p5–p95), 15 levels in use, highest 14; 24% cliff, 33% flat; tallest fall 11 levels.
- **Water:** 4 rivers (2 from the edge, 2 from springs), 1 lake, 5 falls; water covers 15% of the map.
- **Reach:** 11% of the dry land is reached on foot from the start; 89% needs stairs (0 natural ramps, 4 uplands left to stairs).
- **Badwater:** none.
