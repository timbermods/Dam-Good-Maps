## Intention

- The start sits under a cliff, with water below (Kyler's own). **Emerged** (15 tiles 2+ levels above within 7, cliff yes, water 8 tiles' walk).
- A lake high on the heights spills over a fall. **Dropped** (no lake on the heights with a fall).

## How it plays

- You start by a lake, 8 tiles away on your level.
- It lasts through the first drought; in a 26-day Hard drought it is gone by day 10.
- A 3-tile dam 16 tiles north-west holds a drought's water.
- Badwater lies 51 tiles north-west.
- You start under a cliff, with your water below.
- Expand to the east and north, on some level land.
- Most of the high ground needs stairs.
- Further out: mine sites, a geothermal field, relics, waterfalls and most of the ruins.

## Terrain

- **Land:** mesaField, escarpment, caldera, basin, mesa over land with no one regional slope; recipe: great-scarp.
- **Relief:** 15 levels (p5–p95), 13 levels in use, highest 16; 36% cliff, 38% flat; tallest fall 5 levels.
- **Water:** 7 rivers (1 from the edge, 6 from springs), 1 lake, 7 falls; water covers 10% of the map.
- **Reach:** 12% of the dry land is reached on foot from the start; 88% needs stairs (8 natural ramps, 2 uplands left to stairs).
- **Badwater:** a hollow on high ground, draining by its own ditch.

## Name

**Crater Shore**, by the names study's rule `crater-shore`: A raised rim encloses a lake basin with one outlet. Hold the outlet to control storage, then settle the inner shore.
