## Intention

- A lake high on the heights spills over a fall. **Emerged** (a 904-tile lake 7 levels above the map's middle spills over a fall).

## How it plays

- You start above water: a pump shore 6 tiles' walk away, down a slope.
- In the first drought it is gone by day 1: store water before it.
- A 5-tile dam 11 tiles east holds a drought's water.
- Badwater lies 32 tiles north.
- 552 logs of wood within 20 tiles' walk, mostly oak: plenty of wood, slow to regrow; plus about 110 logs growing.
- A lake high on the heights spills over a fall.
- Expand to the west and south-west, on narrow ledges.
- Most of the high ground needs stairs.
- Further out: mine sites, a geothermal field, relics, other lakes and rivers, waterfalls and most of the ruins.

## Terrain

- **Land:** basin, caldera, plateau over land falling toward the west; recipe: island-in-a-river.
- **Relief:** 12 levels (p5–p95), 13 levels in use, highest 16; 20% cliff, 62% flat; tallest fall 4.88 levels.
- **Water:** 5 rivers (1 from the edge, 4 from springs), 2 lakes, 6 falls, a river island; water covers 11% of the map.
- **Reach:** 9% of the dry land is reached on foot from the start; 91% needs stairs (7 natural ramps, 6 uplands left to stairs).
- **Badwater:** a hollow on high ground, draining by its own ditch.
