## Intention

- A lake high on the heights spills over a fall. **Emerged** (a 2460-tile lake 6 levels above the map's middle spills over a fall).

## How it plays

- You start by a lake, right beside you.
- In the first drought it is gone by day 1: store water before it.
- A 1-tile dam 15 tiles north-east holds a drought's water.
- Badwater lies 29 tiles north-west, and some reaches your water.
- A lake high on the heights spills over a fall.
- Expand to the east and west, on some level land.
- Most of the high ground needs stairs.
- Further out: mine sites, a geothermal field, relics and most of the ruins.

## Terrain

- **Land:** trough, basin, mesaField, mesa over land with no one regional slope.
- **Relief:** 10 levels (p5–p95), 15 levels in use, highest 16; 26% cliff, 48% flat; tallest fall 8.38 levels.
- **Water:** 5 rivers (0 from the edge, 5 from springs), 3 lakes, 3 falls; water covers 16% of the map.
- **Reach:** 10% of the dry land is reached on foot from the start; 90% needs stairs (3 natural ramps, 2 uplands left to stairs).
- **Badwater:** a hollow on high ground, draining by its own ditch.

## Name

**Poolside Valley**, by the names study's rule `poolside-valley`: A natural pool sits beside the river valley. Use its shore to expand while saving enough water for drought.
