## Intention

- The start looks out from high ground over the land below. **Dropped** (start at level 4 (map's 75th percentile 7), 4 levels above the lowest ground within 15 tiles).
- The best farmland lies past the gorge. **Emerged** (406 tiles of farmland past a gorge, against 200 by the start).

## How it plays

- You start by a river, right beside you.
- In the first drought it is gone by day 1: store water before it.
- A 17-tile dam 14 tiles north holds a drought's water.
- Badwater lies 37 tiles west.
- The best farmland lies across the gorge: bridge it to grow.
- Expand to the west and south, on narrow ledges.
- Most of the high ground needs stairs.
- Further out: mine sites, a geothermal field, relics, another lake or river, waterfalls and most of the ruins.

## Terrain

- **Land:** plateau, basin, escarpment over land with no one regional slope.
- **Relief:** 11 levels (p5–p95), 14 levels in use, highest 13; 7% cliff, 56% flat; tallest fall 5 levels.
- **Water:** 3 rivers (0 from the edge, 3 from springs), 0 lakes, 2 falls, a delta; water covers 4% of the map.
- **Reach:** 1% of the dry land is reached on foot from the start; 99% needs stairs (2 natural ramps, 0 uplands left to stairs).
- **Badwater:** a hollow on high ground, draining by its own ditch.
