## Intention

- Two rivers meet by the start. **Emerged** (a confluence 13 tiles from the start).
- A waterfall shields the start: its cliff stands between the start and the nearest threat. **Dropped** (a fall 18 tiles away; the threat 9 tiles off is 5 tiles' walk).

## How it plays

- You start by a lake, 8 tiles away on your level.
- It lasts through the first drought; in a 26-day Hard drought it is gone by day 18.
- A 2-tile dam 12 tiles north holds a drought's water.
- Badwater lies 8 tiles east, and some reaches your water.
- Two rivers meet by the start.
- Expand to the west and south, on narrow ledges.
- Most of the high ground needs stairs.
- Further out: mine sites, a geothermal field, relics, another lake or river, waterfalls and most of the ruins.

## Terrain

- **Land:** cone, mesaField, ridge, basin over land falling toward the north-west; recipe: island-in-a-river.
- **Relief:** 15 levels (p5–p95), 17 levels in use, highest 16; 32% cliff, 29% flat; tallest fall 6.09 levels.
- **Water:** 6 rivers (2 from the edge, 4 from springs), 3 lakes, 5 falls, a river island; water covers 15% of the map.
- **Reach:** 6% of the dry land is reached on foot from the start; 95% needs stairs (1 natural ramp, 6 uplands left to stairs).
- **Badwater:** a hollow on high ground, draining by its own ditch.

## Name

**Lake Steps**, by the names study's rule `lake-steps`: A chain of lakes descends through short channels. Secure the upper outlets first; each lower basin drains sooner.
