## Intention

- The start looks out from high ground over the land below. **Emerged** (start at level 15 (map's 75th percentile 11), 14 levels above the lowest ground within 15 tiles).
- A waterfall plunges off a cliff into a large, roughly round lake (Kyler's own). **Dropped** (a lake of 2876 tiles of open water (axes 0.44, fill 0.36) with a fall of 8.1 levels into it).

## How it plays

- You start by a lake, 7 tiles away on your level.
- In the first drought it is gone by day 1: store water before it.
- A 5-tile dam 15 tiles north-west holds a drought's water.
- 298 logs of wood within 20 tiles' walk, mixed woods; plus about 120 logs growing.
- You start on high ground, looking out over the land below.
- Expand to the east, north and south, on narrow ledges.
- Further out: mine sites, a geothermal field, relics, other lakes and rivers, waterfalls and most of the ruins.

## Terrain

- **Land:** cone, basin, mesa, escarpment over a bowl draining toward the north-west.
- **Relief:** 14 levels (p5–p95), 16 levels in use, highest 15; 24% cliff, 48% flat; tallest fall 8.13 levels.
- **Water:** 5 rivers (3 from the edge, 2 from springs), 3 lakes, 5 falls; water covers 26% of the map.
- **Reach:** 15% of the dry land is reached on foot from the start; 85% needs stairs (1 natural ramp, 8 uplands left to stairs).
- **Badwater:** none.
