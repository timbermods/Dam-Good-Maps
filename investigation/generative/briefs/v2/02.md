## Intention

- A snaking river winds down a hill, dropping a level at its bends (Kyler's own). **Emerged** (a river turns 3 times, back and forth, over 30 tiles while its bed drops 12 levels (a level dropped at 2 bends)).

## How it plays

- You start by a lake, 4 tiles away on your level.
- It lasts through the first drought; in a 26-day Hard drought it is gone by day 6.
- A 15-tile dam by the start holds a drought's water.
- Badwater lies 28 tiles east.
- A river snakes down a hill, dropping a level at its bends.
- Expand to the north-west, west, south-west and south-east, on narrow ledges.
- Most of the high ground needs stairs.
- Further out: mine sites, a geothermal field, relics, other lakes and rivers, waterfalls and most of the ruins.

## Terrain

- **Land:** trough, plateau, basin over land with no one regional slope.
- **Relief:** 14 levels (p5–p95), 17 levels in use, highest 16; 11% cliff, 25% flat; tallest fall 1.04 levels.
- **Water:** 4 rivers (1 from the edge, 3 from springs), 5 lakes, 0 falls; water covers 11% of the map.
- **Reach:** 12% of the dry land is reached on foot from the start; 88% needs stairs (1 natural ramp, 1 upland left to stairs).
- **Badwater:** a hollow on high ground, draining by its own ditch.
