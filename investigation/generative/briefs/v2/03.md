## Intention

- A large crater gathers two or more rivers into its lake, which leaves through a gap in the rim (Kyler's own). **Emerged** (a 403-tile lake: rim on 13 of 16 rays (falling again outside on 10), 2 rivers in, 1 way out).

## How it plays

- You start by a stream, 3 tiles away on your level.
- In the first drought it is gone by day 1: store water before it.
- A 4-tile dam 25 tiles south-west holds a drought's water.
- Badwater lies 58 tiles west.
- 340 logs of wood within 20 tiles' walk, mostly oak: plenty of wood, slow to regrow; plus about 150 logs growing.
- Rivers meet in a crater lake that spills out through one gap in its rim.
- Expand to the east, north and south, on narrow ledges.
- Further out: mine sites, a geothermal field, relics, other lakes and rivers, waterfalls and most of the ruins.

## Terrain

- **Land:** ridge, caldera, basin, escarpment over land with no one regional slope; recipe: great-scarp.
- **Relief:** 13 levels (p5–p95), 16 levels in use, highest 16; 12% cliff, 38% flat; tallest fall 3 levels.
- **Water:** 4 rivers (1 from the edge, 3 from springs), 3 lakes, 2 falls, a river island; water covers 13% of the map.
- **Reach:** 23% of the dry land is reached on foot from the start; 77% needs stairs (1 natural ramp, 2 uplands left to stairs).
- **Badwater:** a hollow on high ground, draining by its own ditch.

## Name

**Crater Shore**, by the names study's rule `crater-shore`: A raised rim encloses a lake basin with one outlet. Hold the outlet to control storage, then settle the inner shore.
