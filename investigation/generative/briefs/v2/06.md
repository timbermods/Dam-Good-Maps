## Intention

- A lake high on the heights spills over a fall. **Emerged** (a 86-tile lake 5 levels above the map's middle spills over a fall).
- A waterfall shields the start: its cliff stands between the start and the nearest threat. **Emerged** (a fall 9 tiles away; the threat 23 tiles off is out of reach on foot).

## How it plays

- You start by a lake, 2 tiles away on your level.
- It lasts through the first drought; in a 26-day Hard drought it is gone by day 7.
- A 1-tile dam 20 tiles south-west holds a drought's water.
- Badwater lies 22 tiles north.
- A lake high on the heights spills over a fall.
- A waterfall's cliff stands between you and the nearest threat.
- Expand to the south-west, south and south-east, on narrow ledges.
- Most of the high ground needs stairs.
- Further out: mine sites, a geothermal field, relics, other lakes and rivers and most of the ruins.

## Terrain

- **Land:** basin, caldera, cone, mesa over a bowl draining toward the north-west.
- **Relief:** 13 levels (p5–p95), 16 levels in use, highest 15; 10% cliff, 31% flat; tallest fall 4.86 levels.
- **Water:** 2 rivers (1 from the edge, 1 from springs), 3 lakes, 2 falls; water covers 10% of the map.
- **Reach:** 3% of the dry land is reached on foot from the start; 98% needs stairs (0 natural ramps, 1 upland left to stairs).
- **Badwater:** a hollow on high ground, draining by its own ditch.
