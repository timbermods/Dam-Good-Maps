## Intention

- Two rivers meet by the start. **Dropped** (no confluence within 18 tiles).
- The best farmland lies past the gorge. **Emerged** (474 tiles of farmland past a gorge, against 251 by the start).

## How it plays

- You start by a river, 10 tiles away on your level.
- It lasts through the first drought; in a 26-day Hard drought it is gone by day 9.
- No short dam holds a drought's water near the start: build levees or dig a reservoir.
- Thorns bar the way north-east, 31 tiles out.
- The best farmland lies across the gorge: bridge it to grow.
- Expand to the east and south, on narrow ledges.
- Further out: mine sites, a geothermal field, relics, another lake or river, waterfalls and most of the ruins.

## Terrain

- **Land:** trough, mesa, basin, cone over land with no one regional slope; recipe: badwater-volcano.
- **Relief:** 12 levels (p5–p95), 9 levels in use, highest 15; 37% cliff, 44% flat; tallest fall 5.05 levels.
- **Water:** 6 rivers (1 from the edge, 5 from springs), 0 lakes, 5 falls; water covers 6% of the map.
- **Reach:** 47% of the dry land is reached on foot from the start; 53% needs stairs (8 natural ramps, 5 uplands left to stairs).
- **Badwater:** a hollow on high ground, draining by its own ditch.

## Name

**Badwater Cone**, by the names study's rule `badwater-cone`: A cone frames a contained badwater basin. Keep colonies on the clean side while you manage its leveed outlet.
