## How it plays

- You start by a river, right beside you.
- In the first drought it is gone by day 1: store water before it.
- A 17-tile dam by the start holds a drought's water.
- Badwater lies 39 tiles south-west, and some reaches your water.
- 243 logs of wood within 20 tiles' walk, mixed woods; plus about 140 logs growing.
- Expand to the east and north, on some level land.
- Further out: mine sites, a geothermal field, relics, waterfalls and most of the ruins.

## Terrain

- **Land:** mesa, basin over land falling toward the south-east.
- **Relief:** 13 levels (p5–p95), 10 levels in use, highest 16; 37% cliff, 46% flat; tallest fall 8.96 levels.
- **Water:** 1 river (0 from the edge, 1 from springs), 2 lakes, 3 falls; water covers 4% of the map.
- **Reach:** 26% of the dry land is reached on foot from the start; 74% needs stairs (8 natural ramps, 1 upland left to stairs).
- **Badwater:** a hollow on high ground, draining by its own ditch.

## Name

**Dryroot Basin**, by the names study's rule `dryroot-basin`: The basin has little standing water and few living trees. Secure a reservoir early before expanding into the drier ground.
