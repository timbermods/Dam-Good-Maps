## How it plays

- You start by a lake, 4 tiles away on your level.
- It lasts through the first drought; in a 26-day Hard drought it is gone by day 8.
- A 3-tile dam 32 tiles west holds a drought's water.
- Thorns bar the way south, 22 tiles out.
- Expand to the north-west, south and south-east, on some level land.
- Further out: mine sites, relics, other lakes and rivers and most of the ruins.

## Terrain

- **Land:** escarpment, plateau, ridge, basin over land with no one regional slope.
- **Relief:** 14 levels (p5–p95), 13 levels in use, highest 16; 36% cliff, 40% flat; tallest fall 4.1 levels.
- **Water:** 4 rivers (2 from the edge, 2 from springs), 2 lakes, 4 falls; water covers 13% of the map.
- **Reach:** 26% of the dry land is reached on foot from the start; 74% needs stairs (8 natural ramps, 5 uplands left to stairs).
- **Badwater:** a hollow on high ground, draining by its own ditch.

## Name

**Poolside Valley**, by the names study's rule `poolside-valley`: A natural pool sits beside the river valley. Use its shore to expand while saving enough water for drought.
